Hardware Setup

  • Size & Color: Use the top dropdowns to match your physical e-paper display parameters before designing.
  • Orientation: Landscape images are automatically rotated behind the scenes to fit portrait hardware.
  • Connection: Keep the keychain powered. Tap "Send" and select Tech_Talkies_ePaper.

Privacy & Security

100% Local Processing.

This web application is completely self-contained. It does not send any of your images, drawings, text, or QR data to any servers or clouds.
